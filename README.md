# 📊 From Data to Decisions: Online Retail Sales Storytelling

## 🚀 Project Overview
Dive into the **Online Retail dataset (2010–2011)** to uncover trends, top products, customer behavior, and regional performance.  
This project uses **data storytelling** to turn millions of transactions into actionable insights for business strategy.

---

## 🔑 Key Insights

### 1. Seasonal Trends
- 🎄 **Sales peak in December**, highlighting holiday shopping patterns.  
- Weekly and monthly trends reveal **slow periods** and **growth opportunities**.

### 2. Top Products
- **WHITE HANGING HEART T-LIGHT HOLDER** & **CREAM CUPID HEARTS COAT HANGER** are consistent best-sellers.  
- Shows **customer preferences** and **seasonal favorites**.

### 3. Customer Behavior
- **Average Order Value** identifies spending patterns 💰  
- **Repeat purchases** show loyalty  
- Outliers indicate **bulk orders or unusual purchases**

### 4. Regional Insights
- 🇬🇧 United Kingdom drives most sales  
- Other countries reveal **emerging potential** for marketing and expansion

### 5. Anomalies & Surprises
- Negative quantities show **returns** 🔄  
- Sudden spikes uncover **unexpected buying behavior**
